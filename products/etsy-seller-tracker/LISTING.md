**Description**

```
See what you really keep from every Etsy sale. Enter your orders, and this spreadsheet takes off every Etsy UK fee, VAT on those fees, your materials and your postage, then shows profit per product and per month.

Works in Excel, Google Sheets, Apple Numbers and LibreOffice. No macros, no subscription.

WHAT YOU GET
• The tracker (.xlsx), ready to use
• An example copy filled in with a year of a candle shop's orders
• A PDF setup guide

INSIDE (7 TABS)
• Settings: Etsy UK fee rates (June 2026), all editable, and whether you've given Etsy a VAT number
• Products: materials and packaging cost per unit, up to 200 products
• Sales: transaction, processing, regulatory, listing and Offsite Ads fees plus VAT on fees, worked out on every line
• Expenses: Etsy Ads, bulk supplies, software, insurance
• Profit per Product: units, revenue, fees, cost and margin for each product
• Monthly Summary: your year month by month
• VAT threshold check: rolling 12-month sales against £90,000, with a warning within £15,000

GOOD TO KNOW
• Fees are worked out from Etsy's published UK rates, so they can differ by pennies from your payment account. Update the rates in Settings if Etsy changes them.
• A record-keeping tool, not tax advice. For tax return totals, see the Etsy Seller Tax Kit bundle.
• Not affiliated with or endorsed by Etsy.

HOW IT ARRIVES
Instant download. You need Excel, Google Sheets (free), Apple Numbers or LibreOffice (free).

For your own shop; please don't share or resell the files.
```
