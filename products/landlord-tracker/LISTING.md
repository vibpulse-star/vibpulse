**Description**

```
Keep the rent and costs for up to 5 UK properties in one spreadsheet, with your share worked out for jointly owned lets, and your Making Tax Digital quarterly totals ready when each quarter ends.

Built for UK landlords for the 2026-27 tax year. Works in Excel, Google Sheets, Apple Numbers and LibreOffice. No macros, no subscription.

WHAT YOU GET
• The tracker (.xlsx), ready to use
• An example copy filled in with two properties
• A PDF setup guide

INSIDE (7 TABS)
• Start Here: set up in 3 steps
• Settings: up to 5 properties and your % share of each
• Income: rent and other property income, quarter filled in from the date
• Expenses: choose the SA105 line from a dropdown
• Quarterly Summary: your share for each quarter and the year to date, with due dates
• Per Property: income, costs and profit for each let
• Year End: SA105-style totals, mortgage interest kept apart, a £1,000 property allowance check and a Making Tax Digital check

GOOD TO KNOW
• Mortgage interest is kept out of profit, because it gives a 20% tax reduction rather than a deduction.
• Making Tax Digital for Income Tax started 6 April 2026 for qualifying income over £50,000 (property and self-employment together), then £30,000 from April 2027 and £20,000 from April 2028.
• A spreadsheet can't submit to HMRC on its own. Use these figures with HMRC-recognised bridging or MTD software.
• A record-keeping tool, not tax advice. It does not work out the tax you owe.
• Not for limited companies or overseas property.

HOW IT ARRIVES
Instant download. You need Excel, Google Sheets (free), Apple Numbers or LibreOffice (free).

Rules are as published for the 2026-27 tax year. For your own use; please don't share or resell the files.
```
